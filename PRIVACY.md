# Privacy Policy: Quick Snippets for Thunderbird

Effective November 23, 2026. Publisher: KING HOLDINGS VENTURES LLC (King AI). Contact: hello@usekingai.com

Quick Snippets for Thunderbird does not collect, transmit, sell or share any data. The extension has no servers, no analytics, no accounts and makes no network requests.

What it stores: the snippets you write (shortcut and text), the name you set for the {name} variable, and your Pro key if you enter one. All of it lives in the extension's local storage (`storage.local`) on your computer. Removing the extension deletes it.

What it reads: while you write a message, a script in the message editor watches the text you type only to notice a shortcut such as ;sig followed by Space or Tab. It does not read, store or send your emails, contacts or messages. The popup's fallback insert reads the message you are writing once, only to add the chosen snippet to it.

Permissions: `storage` keeps your snippets on your computer. `compose` lets the extension work in the message-writing window.

Pro key: the key is checked on your computer. It is never sent anywhere. The "Get Pro" link opens a Stripe payment page in your default browser only when you click it.

Questions or deletion requests: hello@usekingai.com
